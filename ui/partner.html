What the finished rows show

Score is 0–100 where 100 is clean. At or below 50 is a severe gap; anything under 100 is worth approaching. A site already running a consent platform scores 100 however many trackers still slip through.

Rows finished per day

Counted from the rows themselves (done or failed), against a daily quota. Rows without a website are skipped and never counted. A row waiting on a retry does not count until it settles.

Queue

queued not yet touched · running claimed by a run in progress · retry a page failed to load, tried again after 6h (3 attempts) · failed gave up · no_website the partner gave no website — skipped, not counted toward the daily quota.
